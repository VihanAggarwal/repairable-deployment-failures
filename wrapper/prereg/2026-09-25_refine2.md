this commit: `pose_sources.estimate("refine2")` refines the silhouette pose EasyHeC-style (dense soft-mask loss, Adam,
finite-difference gradient, blur 4/2/1 px, 30 steps each) from two starts, the silhouette pose and the training pose,

pose and its loss is below 0.8 x the loss at the training pose. Disclosed: the calibration error of learned masks +
refinement was measured locally and policy-free on set A before this file (vendor/local_refine_learned.py); the
